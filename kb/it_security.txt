Zenvora IT Security Guidelines

# Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include upper case, lower case, a number and a symbol. Passwords expire every 90 days and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff.

# Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR portal. Use the Zenvora Authenticator app. SMS codes are not accepted.

# VPN Usage
The company VPN must be used when accessing internal systems from outside the office. Connect using the GlobalProtect client with your corporate credentials. VPN sessions disconnect after 30 minutes of inactivity.

# Laptop and Device Rules
Company laptops must have disk encryption enabled and may not be used by family members. Installing unapproved software is prohibited. If a laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk at helpdesk@zenvora.example or extension 4040 within 2 hours so the device can be remotely wiped.

# Reporting Security Incidents
Phishing emails, malware alerts and suspected data breaches must be reported to security@zenvora.example immediately. Do not delete the suspicious email; use the Report Phishing button. The Security team responds within 1 hour.

# Email and Data Handling
Confidential data must not be sent to personal email accounts. USB storage devices are blocked by default.
