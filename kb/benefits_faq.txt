Zenvora Benefits FAQ

# Health Insurance
All employees are covered under a group health insurance policy of INR 5,00,000 from the first day of employment. Spouse, children and parents can be added during enrollment at a nominal premium.

# Provident Fund
Both the employee and the employer contribute 12 percent of basic salary to the provident fund.

# Learning Allowance
Each employee gets INR 30,000 per year for courses and certifications. Courses must be relevant to the role and approved by the manager.

# Employee Referral Bonus
A referral bonus of INR 25,000 is paid after the referred candidate completes 3 months in the company.

# Salary Payment
Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.

# Probation and Notice
The probation period is 3 months. After confirmation the notice period is 60 days.
